# JuliaCon presentation

A 16:9 Beamer deck (metropolis theme) covering the CDC paper for a JuliaCon
audience. It assumes no experimental-design background: notation, posterior and
expected information gain are introduced from scratch before the bioreactor
appears. The implementation stack (Lux, Reactant, Enzyme) and the JAX comparison
get more room than they do in the paper. The sPCE bound is named rather than
derived, the target/nuisance split is one small-print remark on frame 4, and there
is no reference list: attribution lives in the paper.

## Build

```bash
make -C presentation           # -> presentation/slides.pdf
make -C presentation render    # -> presentation/build/rendered/*.png (1920 wide)
```

pdfLaTeX only. Figures come from `../paper/figures/`, produced by the Snakemake
pipeline.

## Structure

| Frames | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 | Title |
| 2–7 | Notation and goal, the bioreactor, the entropy criterion, nested Monte Carlo, static schedules to policies, what one gradient step costs |
| 8–13 | The Julia stack and what it costs, the policy in Lux, the objective under Reactant, the same estimator in JAX, the benchmark contract |
| 14–18 | Monod rollouts, Monod posteriors and scores, Haldane inhibition, the DC motor in real time, code and paper (QR) |

`speaker_notes.md` has per-frame notes, pacing (~25 minutes over 18 slides), the
split of ownership between the two halves, and the list of items still open.

The results slides are figure-first: minimal text, one takeaway line each. The PK
example, the limitations slide and the reproduction details were dropped from the
talk; they are still in the paper. The QR code on the last frame is generated by
the `qrcode` LaTeX package, so there is no image asset to keep in sync.

## Benchmark slide

Frame 13 has an empty results table on purpose. Draft numbers exist on
`origin/smc/jax-reactant-benchmark`, but the two harnesses do not yet time the
same region; `benchmark_handoff.md` records the branch, the draft values, the
metric contract, and the harness changes needed before the numbers can be shown.
